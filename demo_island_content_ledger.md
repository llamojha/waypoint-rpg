# Demo Island — Regions, POIs, NPCs

## Regions overview

| Region | Location on island | Theme | Biome / region description |
|---|---|---|---|
| The Highlands | central / uplands | highlands anchor | Upland core region with a named mountain (The Needleback) and the island’s central landmark (The Waystone). |
| Stormwall Coast | north | Aran Islands-inspired glacio-karst | Glacio-karst coastline: exposed rock, limestone features, harsh wind, cold surf. Flotsam economy anchored by a tavern-storage hub. |
| Caledonia | west–south coast | Sark-inspired | Coastal region with a port capital, a wind corridor isthmus, a windmill landmark, and a wilderness interior. |
| Dunamar | south and east | La Graciosa-inspired | Arid region: bushes, dry soil, huge sand dunes, isolated hills, and a cave system for exploration. |
